Scatter plot of word count reduction (%) vs perplexity under the unsteered Qwen3-0.6B model, colored by alpha value (0.0 to 3.0). At alpha=1.5-2.0, outputs achieve 50-80% compression while maintaining perplexity below 50 (coherent). At alpha >= 3.0, perplexity spikes above 200, indicating coherence collapse. Demonstrates that terse steering compresses output without generating garbage up to the collapse threshold.
