Rank-biserial effect sizes with Bonferroni-corrected significance stars for all pairwise direction comparisons across three surface features. Terse vs baseline and terse vs verbose show near-perfect separation (r=0.96-1.00, p<.01), but baseline vs verbose is moderate and non-significant (r=0.40-0.46), confirming that the terse screen is the strong signal while the verbose screen barely separates from unsteered output in surface statistics.
