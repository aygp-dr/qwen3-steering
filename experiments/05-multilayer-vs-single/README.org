#+TITLE: Experiment 05: Multi-Layer vs Single-Layer Steering
#+DATE: 2026-03-06
#+STATUS: pending

* Goal

Test CPRR-6: does distributing the same total steering pressure across
multiple layers produce a better terseness-to-coherence ratio than
concentrating it at one layer?

* Method

5 configurations at equal total pressure (2.0):

| Config | Layers          | Alpha each | Total |
|--------+-----------------+------------+-------|
| A      | L12             |        2.0 |   2.0 |
| B      | L12 + L16       |        1.0 |   2.0 |
| C      | L10 + L14 + L18 |       0.67 |   2.0 |
| D      | L12 + L15       |        1.0 |   2.0 |
| E      | L12 (control)   |        1.0 |   1.0 |

8 questions x 6 configs (including baseline) = 48 generations.
Composite score: (1/words) x topic x (1/ppl) x 1000.

* Predictions

- Multi-layer (B or C) outperforms single (A) on composite score
- Adjacent layers (D) may interfere, worse than spaced layers (B)

* CPRR Conjectures

| ID | Claim                              | Status  |
|----+------------------------------------+---------|
|  6 | Multi-layer outperforms single     | testing |
