* Key Findings Summary

- *Unit normalization destroys steering* (C-26 refuted, C-27 proved): Raw activation
  differences at alpha=2.0 produce 8% residual perturbation. Unit norm at alpha=0.20
  produces 0.04% — zero effect.

- *Style vectors are NOT orthogonal* (H-SV-1 proved): formal/socratic/dry-wit cluster
  at cos_sim>0.95. Terse is the anti-direction at -0.70. At d_model=1024 there's really
  only one style axis: "elaborate more" vs "be terse."

- *Surface features partially recover screens* (C-28 preliminary): K-means on word count
  recovers terse cleanly but cannot separate baseline from verbose (ARI=0.395 at n=10).
  Full 100-prompt run pending.

- *Complex terministic screens need more dimensions* (H-SM-5 evidence): cult_of_jason
  vec norm=189.75 with effective alpha=0.15-0.25. The concept is too complex for 1024
  dimensions without coherence collapse.
