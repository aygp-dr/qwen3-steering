* Part I: Foundations -- Why Steering Vectors Work

** Linear Representation Hypothesis

- [ ] [[https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.03658][The Linear Representation Hypothesis and the Geometry of Large Language Models]] (Park, Choe & Veitch, 2023)
  - Formalizes why =act_pos - act_neg= produces a meaningful direction
- [ ] [[https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.06824][The Geometry of Truth: Emergent Linear Structure in LLM Representations]] (Marks & Tegmark, 2023)
  - Empirical validation: concepts are linearly encoded in activation space
- [ ] [[https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.03867][On the Origins of Linear Representations in Large Language Models]] (Jiang et al., 2024)
  - Proves next-token prediction + gradient descent produces linear representations
- [ ] [[https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.14860][Not All Language Model Features Are Linear]] (Engels et al., 2024)
  - Important caveat: multi-dimensional features exist (circular, categorical)

** Superposition and Polysemanticity

- [ ] [[https://transformer-circuits.pub/2022/toy_model/index.html][Toy Models of Superposition]] (Elhage et al., 2022)
  - Why features are compressed into fewer dimensions; at d_model=1024 superposition is dense
- [ ] [[https://transformer-circuits.pub/2023/monosemantic-features/index.html][Towards Monosemanticity: Decomposing Language Models with Dictionary Learning]] (2023)
  - One-layer transformer SAE feature extraction; foundation for dictionary learning

** Probing and Latent Knowledge

- [ ] [[https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.03827][Discovering Latent Knowledge in Language Models Without Supervision]] (Burns et al., 2022)
  - CCS: unsupervised direction-finding in activation space; ancestor of contrastive steering
- [ ] [[https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.14913][Transformer Feed-Forward Layers Are Key-Value Memories]] (Geva et al., 2021)
  - FFN layers as key-value stores; informs where to inject steering vectors

* Part II: Core Methods -- How to Steer

** Activation Addition (ActAdd)                                    :steering:

- [ ] [[https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.10248][Activation Addition: Steering Language Models Without Optimization]] (Turner et al., 2023)
  - *The ActAdd paper.* Our =actadd.py= implements this method directly.
- [ ] [[https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.06681][Steering Llama 2 via Contrastive Activation Addition]] (Rimsky et al., 2024)
  - Extends ActAdd with systematic layer sweep, alpha sensitivity, coherence curves
- [ ] [[https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.03813][Improving Activation Steering in Language Models with Mean-Centring]] (Jorgensen et al., 2023)
  - Mean-centring improvement; directly relevant to corpus-mean approach

** Inference-Time Intervention                                     :steering:

- [ ] [[https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.03341][Inference-Time Intervention: Eliciting Truthful Answers from a Language Model]] (Li et al., 2023)
  - Probe-based direction-finding + intervention at attention head level; layer selection methodology

** Representation Engineering                                      :steering:

- [ ] [[https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.01405][Representation Engineering: A Top-Down Approach to AI Transparency]] (Zou et al., 2023)
  - Defines the representation reading/writing paradigm; multi-property control
- [ ] [[https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.05124][Extracting Latent Steering Vectors from Pretrained Language Models]] (Subramani et al., 2022)
  - Earlier work on steering vectors from latent space; historical context

** Style Vectors                                                   :steering:

- [ ] [[https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.01618][Style Vectors for Steering Generative Large Language Models]] (Konen et al., EACL 2024)
  - *Our =corpus_style_vec.py= implements this method.* Corpus mean approach with neutral baseline.

** Advanced Steering Methods (2024-2026)                           :steering:

- [ ] [[https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.09631][Representation Surgery: Theory and Practice of Affine Steering]] (Singh & Ravfogel, 2024)
  - When linear steering works vs. when you need affine corrections; alpha threshold theory
- [ ] [[https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.15213][Function Vectors in Large Language Models]] (Todd et al., 2024)
  - Task-level behaviors captured as single vectors; supports style axis approach
- [ ] [[https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.11717][Refusal in Language Models Is Mediated by a Single Direction]] (Arditi et al., 2024)
  - Refusal is linear in the residual stream; test cos_sim of style vectors against refusal direction

** Knowledge Editing (Related)

- [ ] [[https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.05262][Locating and Editing Factual Associations in GPT]] (Meng et al., 2022, ROME)
  - Causal tracing methodology for identifying which layers store what; informs layer sweep
- [ ] [[https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.07229][Mass-Editing Memory in a Transformer]] (Meng et al., 2023, MEMIT)
  - Scaling knowledge editing; rank-one updates vs. activation-time intervention tradeoffs

* Part III: Safety, Evaluation, and Reliability

** Steering Safety and Dual-Use Risks                              :safety:

- [ ] [[https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.04896][Compliance Steering Incidentally Increases Jailbreak Surface]] (2026)
  - Cited in =setup.org=; benign compliance vectors degrade safety alignment
- [ ] [[https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.06681][Steering Llama 2 via Contrastive Activation Addition]] (Rimsky et al., 2024)
  - Documents how CAA at specific layers affects refusal behavior and sycophancy
- [ ] [[https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.03693][Fine-tuning Aligned Language Models Compromises Safety]] (Qi et al., 2023)
  - Any intervention on internal representations can degrade safety; generalizes to steering
- [ ] [[https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.02483][Jailbroken: How Does LLM Safety Training Fail?]] (Wei et al., 2023)
  - Taxonomy of attack surfaces; activation steering as a vector

** Evaluation Methodology                                          :evaluation:

- [ ] [[https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05685][Judging LLM-as-a-Judge with MT-Bench and Chatbot Arena]] (Zheng et al., 2023)
  - LLM-as-judge for automated style quality evaluation
- [ ] [[https://www.anthropic.com/research/evaluating-feature-steering-social-biases][Evaluating feature steering: A case study in mitigating social biases]] (Anthropic, 2024)
  - Steering evaluation methodology: effectiveness vs. side effects
- [ ] [[https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.07958][TruthfulQA: Measuring How Models Mimic Human Falsehoods]] (Lin et al., 2022)
  - Benchmark for measuring factual preservation under steering
- [ ] [[https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.09251][Discovering Language Model Behaviors with Model-Written Evaluations]] (Perez et al., 2022)

** Reliability and Failure Modes                                   :evaluation:

- [ ] [[https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.15154][Linear Representations of Sentiment in Large Language Models]] (Tigges et al., 2023)
  - Linear sentiment representations across model sizes; relevant to 0.6B scale expectations

** Deployment Constraints

Hook-based activation steering is only possible with PyTorch/transformers runtime.
Compiled/quantized formats (GGUF/Ollama, ONNX, CoreML, TensorRT, AWS Neuron) eliminate
hook points. For format-agnostic steering, vectors must be baked into weights directly
(see RepE paper, Section 5).

| Format | Runtime | Hooks? | Notes |
|------------+------------------+--------+---------------------------------------|
| safetensors | transformers | Yes | Current approach; full hook support |
| GGUF | Ollama/llama.cpp | No | No hook API; prompt-level only |
| ONNX | ONNX Runtime | No | Compiled graph; no runtime hooks |
| CoreML | Apple CoreML | No | Compiled graph |
| TensorRT | NVIDIA TRT | No | Compiled engine |
