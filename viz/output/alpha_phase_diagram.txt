Phase diagram of output word count vs alpha scaling factor for activation steering on Qwen3-0.6B. Top panel: three regimes emerge — a dead zone below alpha=0.5 where greedy decoding is unchanged, effective steering from alpha=1.5-2.5 where output drops from 149 to ~16 words, and coherence collapse above alpha=3.0. Bottom panel compares raw vs unit-normalized vectors on a log scale, showing that unit vectors need alpha ~100x larger to achieve the same perturbation.
