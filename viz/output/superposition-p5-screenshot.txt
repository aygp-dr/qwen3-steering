Interactive 3D p5.js visualization of superposition entanglement at d_model=1024. Two helical strands (terse=blue, worldview=amber) intertwine inside a wireframe cylinder whose radius is proportional to sqrt(d_model). At alpha=2.0 (effective steering), the helices show moderate perturbation with white interference spots at crossing points. Sliders control alpha, d_model, and interference strength.
